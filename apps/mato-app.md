### Future video editing is here. Run by an AI agent.

Mato Editor is a free desktop video editor for creating faceless videos. You write what you want, and an AI assistant generates the scenes complete with the voice-over, backgrounds, images, and captions — then places them on the canvas and timeline using the exact same controls you'd use by hand. Every AI decision stays fully editable: move a text layer, retrim a clip, swap a voice, undo anything. Organize work into channels and videos, then export a finished MP4.

#### Highlights
- Completely free to use
- Use your own API key for AI generation or use Mato credits
- Many choices of natural AI voiceovers, or use your own trained AI voice
- Collection of many art styles to use, or create your own artstyle
- Create consistent characters
- Built-in media library to browse thousand of free royalti background music and sound effects
